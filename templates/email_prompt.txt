You are an expert email writer. A university student wants to send a professional email to various IT companies requesting an industrial attachment (internship).

Your task is to compose a personalized email for the specific company being contacted, using the student's profile details below.

STUDENT PROFILE:
- Name: {student_name}
- University: {university}
- Course: {course}
- Year: {year}
- Skills: {skills}
- Interests: {interests}
- Attachment Period: {attachment_start} to {attachment_end} ({attachment_duration})

TARGET COMPANY:
- Company Name: {company_name}
- Industry/Category: {company_category}
- Location: {company_address}

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Write a professional, concise attachment request email (150-250 words).
2. Address it to "Dear Hiring Manager" (or "Dear {company_name} Team" if more appropriate).
3. Mention the student's university, course, and year in the opening paragraph.
4. Highlight 2-3 relevant skills from their profile that match the company's industry.
5. Express genuine interest in the company and what the student hopes to learn.
6. Mention that a CV is attached.
7. Include the full sign-off ("Sincerely,\n\n{student_name}") in your output body.
8. Keep the tone professional, enthusiastic, and respectful — not desperate.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Return ONLY valid JSON with exactly two fields:
{{"subject": "A clear, professional subject line (under 10 words)", "body": "The complete email body text, INCLUDING the sign-off"}}
